# Code of Conduct

This project brings together practitioners, translators and people who write code, from different traditions and countries. We take part with patience, respect and good faith.

## What We Ask

- Treat everyone with courtesy, whatever their tradition, lineage, language, experience or background.
- Discuss the work: the code, the layout, the sources. Disagree with a question and a case, never with an attack on the person.
- Handle sacred texts with care. Copy the Tibetan exactly, keep the translators' credits, and respect any restriction of empowerment or transmission that a source states.
- Accept that a reviewer may ask for evidence, and offer it.

## What We Do Not Accept

- Insults, harassment, mockery of any tradition or teacher, and sexual or violent language.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Using the project, its pechas or its saved pages for profit.

## Reporting a Problem

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer through [github.com/eltonmelo21](https://github.com/eltonmelo21), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue. Reports are read in private. The maintainer may edit or remove content and limit the participation of anyone who does not follow this code.
