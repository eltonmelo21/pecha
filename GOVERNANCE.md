# Governance

**English** · [Português](GOVERNANCE.pt-BR.md) · [繁體中文](GOVERNANCE.zh-Hant.md)

Pecha is a non-profit initiative for the study and practice of the Dharma. It is open to being continued by a foundation, a university or another institution with more resources for artificial intelligence and more time, so that the work can reach more people.

This page tells how the project could be carried on, for anyone who would like to talk about it.

## Conditions

There are three things to look after for anyone who would like to take over the maintenance of the project:

1. The skill and the pechas it makes stay free of charge for everyone who studies and practises.
2. The open licences stay as they are: MIT for the code, the SIL Open Font License (OFL) for the Noto fonts, and the licence of origin for each text, which is CC BY-NC 4.0 for the Lotsawa House pages.
3. The credits stay in place: to the translators, to Lotsawa House, to the Noto fonts and to the author and origin of the project.

## The Author's Role

In a handover, the author passes on the maintenance and stays close by, in an advisory role, to talk through decisions and to review visual changes to the pecha before each release.

## Contact

To talk about continuing the project, open a conversation in the [Partnerships](https://github.com/eltonmelo21/pecha/discussions/categories/partnerships) category of this repository's Discussions.

## What the Skill Does Today

- Lays out a Lotsawa House page as a PDF to print, with two pechas of 297 × 105 mm on each A4 landscape sheet, a double frame, Tibetan page numbers, a cover and a credits page.
- Reads the Tibetan, transliteration and translation as the page marks them, with no AI reading of the text. The same page with the same options gives the same PDF.
- Lets the person choose the layers, single or double-sided printing, the frame lines and how the translator's headings are placed.
- Converts other sources (another site, a translation the person gives) with the help of AI, and asks the person to check the text before the PDF is made.
- Marks every pecha as prepared with the help of AI, to be reviewed before study and practice.

## What Is Still Open

- Example pechas reviewed by practitioners and translators.
- A step-by-step guide for people who have never used AI.
- Reading sources other than Lotsawa House without an AI conversion.
- Testing with a wider set of texts than the reference texts used today.

None of these has a date. They stay open for anyone who would like to help and is able to.

## Text Sources

Today the skill is designed for Lotsawa House pages, whose markup separates the Tibetan, the transliteration and the translation. It is this markup that lets the skill read the pages without AI. The project is independent and was born with gratitude for the work of Lotsawa House and its translators. It is also made to serve that work, bringing the texts to paper for those who practise.

The CC BY-NC 4.0 licence that Lotsawa House uses allows non-commercial derivative work, which fits the purpose of this project. Other translation sources may have different licences and are looked at case by case; some may ask for permission before any derivative work.

The project is not limited to one source. Other projects that translate Tibetan texts, such as 84000, Khyentse Vision Project and Samye Translations, are possible examples of future studies, if they are available to collaborate. Anyone who translates and would like to talk about this is very welcome in this repository's [Discussions](https://github.com/eltonmelo21/pecha/discussions).

In the end, this is a non-profit initiative, made to benefit those who practise.
