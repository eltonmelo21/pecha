# Contributing to Pecha

Thank you for wanting to help. This project serves the study and practice of the Dharma, without profit.

## Reporting a Problem

If a pecha comes out wrong, or this guide does not match what you see in Claude, open an [issue](https://github.com/eltonmelo21/pecha/issues) with:

- the address of the Lotsawa House page;
- the sentence you sent to Claude;
- the version of the skill, from the [Releases](https://github.com/eltonmelo21/pecha/releases) page;
- what you expected and what came out, with the page of the PDF and whether the pecha is at the top or at the bottom of the sheet.

## Starting a Conversation

For questions, ideas or translations, open a conversation in this repository's [Discussions](https://github.com/eltonmelo21/pecha/discussions). Institutions interested in carrying the project on can write in the [Partnerships](https://github.com/eltonmelo21/pecha/discussions/categories/partnerships) category.
