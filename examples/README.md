# Example Pechas

Twelve pechas made by this skill, to show what it gives before you install it, with each choice of frame. Each one was laid out from a saved page of [Lotsawa House](https://www.lotsawahouse.org), printed single-sided. Seven are in three layers (Tibetan, transliteration and translation), and the Seven Branches and the Auspicious Verses of Seven Buddhas are in transliteration and translation, all nine with two thin frame lines. The Praises to the Twenty-One Tārās and The Sūtra of Boundless Life are in Tibetan only, as one continuous text: the Praises to the Twenty-One Tārās has the thick line outside the frame, and the Sūtra of Boundless Life has it inside. The Treasury of Blessings is in two blocks: all the Tibetan first, then the transliteration and translation, with two thin frame lines.

> [!WARNING]
> These are **unreviewed examples**. They were checked page by page for layout, but no practitioner or translator has reviewed them for study and practice. Every pecha says on its last page that it was prepared with the help of artificial intelligence and must be reviewed before use.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Only texts that Lotsawa House presents as open, without a requirement of empowerment or transmission, are included.
