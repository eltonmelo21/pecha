# Ideas for the Future

**English** · [Português](futuro.md)

These are ideas for the next stages of the project. None of them has been tested yet. They are recorded here so that anyone who wants to take part can see where the work could go.

## Other AI Models

The skill was tested in Claude. The next step is to test it on other platforms, such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok, and see whether the pechas come out the same.

## Other Translation Sources

Today the skill reads the pages of Lotsawa House. Other translation groups also publish Tibetan texts with their translations. In the future, those texts could be read in the same way, always with the credits and the licence of each source.

## Other Languages

The translations used so far are in English. The skill could also be tested with translations into other languages, keeping the Tibetan exactly as the source gives it.

## Chinese Transliteration and Translation

There may be demand for pechas with transliteration and translation in Chinese. This needs its own tests, from the reading of the source to the fonts and the layout of the page.

## Drawings on the Covers

The opening and closing covers could carry black and white line drawings, as many printed pechas do.

## Cut Line

Today the cut line always comes in the PDF: on every sheet when single-sided, and only on the front when double-sided. One idea is to let the person choose whether the line appears. Another is to replace the full line with two short marks, one on each side of the sheet, outside the text, so that nothing shows after cutting.

## How to Take Part

Buddhist institutions, foundations and universities that would like to help with any of these ideas are very welcome. How an institution can take part is in [Governance](../GOVERNANCE.md), and the conversation can start in the [Partnerships](https://github.com/eltonmelo21/pecha/discussions/categories/partnerships) category of this repository's Discussions.
